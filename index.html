Life has carried me through many chapters and many places, each one leaving something behind in how I think and what I care about. The latest stop is Sunnyvale, CA — and the newest chapter is the most personal one yet: I recently became a mom, which has quietly deepened everything I already believed about why the work we do matters.

I am deeply committed to creating a positive, meaningful impact in the lives of people. Each of my past experiences has led me to where I am today. I love doing what I do, and I’m grateful to be in a position where I can do this.

I run HyperVerge Academy, a program that upskills young people from under-resourced communities through volunteer mentors and enables them to get a well-paying job in the tech sector.
